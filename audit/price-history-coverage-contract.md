## Source findings and scientific limits

- `AvuParityChart.vue` requests ALL/5Y at 30-day steps, 1Y daily, 30D six-hourly, 7D hourly, 24H ten-minute. Relay `oracle/feed.rs::range` returns the floor point plus the last actual observation in each step bucket, not historical vendor candles. A newly collected MON series can therefore begin as one monthly bucket. The generated seed has prices only for BCH/BTC/DOGE/LTC/XEC/XMR; no MON or gold. Lack of older MON data cannot be repaired with chart interpolation.
- Store `oracle.ts::ensureHistory` requests uncovered intervals/resolutions and merges timestamps. `oracle-series.ts` keeps the last 14 days at full received resolution, older points at one actual observation per UTC day. Thus the same recent 132 observations can truthfully fall inside 7D/30D/1Y; changing a requested range does not create older history. An earlier coordinator update said 30 days of full-resolution cache; the source says 14 days.
- Chart labels formerly used the selected range button, while x coordinates used actual plotted extent. Now both date/time precision and tick positions use visible observations; available first/last and requested window are explicit. Long-window queries remain broad, supplemented by a last-30-day hourly request (720 steps, within relay's 1000-step limit) to recover recent detail from initial monthly bucketing. This does not fabricate pre-collection observations.
- Gold is unfinished functionality: chart `currentRate` and `rangeSeries` explicitly return unavailable/empty for Gold. A Chainlink XAU/USD latest-price provider exists in `packages/price-feeds/src/providers/chainlink.ts`, but the direct adapter does not request GOLD/XAU, the seed contains no gold series, and checked-in relay provider configuration has no gold symbol. Annual World Bank averages exist in a separate historical JSON; they cannot provide dated intraday prices. Wiring a dated gold series and its actual history is a separate data contract, not completed here.
- `packages/wallet/oracle/energy-basket.ts::avuHashAt` sums each basket entry's price × miner reward / (difficulty × hashes-per-difficulty), multiplies by hardware hashes/kWh, inverts to kWh/USD and weights entry inverses by market cap with the configured cap. All inputs use latest-at-or-before lookup. Bundled historical prices, difficulties, circulation and subsidy are monthly observations; curated efficiency/reward steps and changing weights also move the curve. Difficulty alone does not explain the steps. Current Blockchair adapter reads difficulty normally; configured DOGE uses observed 24-hour hashrate × 60 seconds / 2^32 expressed in the same difficulty unit. Default relay price rounds are 600 seconds, chain stats 3600 seconds, with configured time-weighted smoothing. No formula, sampling methodology, backend or generated seed changed.
