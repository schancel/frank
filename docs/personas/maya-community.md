# Maya — keeping up with a community

Fictional working hypothesis; no interview or historical Stamp motivation is represented.

Maya has heard about Frank from a small community. She is curious about private messaging and
recognizes cryptocurrency terms, but does not administer software or troubleshoot wallets.
She wants to know whether the people and conversations she cares about are here.

Her possible motivation is staying connected with less unwanted contact. Whether that matters
more than familiarity, cost, or convenience is a question for a real trial.

## Normal tasks

- Find a known person and exchange a short message.
- Recognize who sent an invitation, then accept or decline it.
- Enter an invited group, read its conversation, and post a reply.
- Return later and find the right conversation and unread messages.

## Observable acceptance on mobile and desktop

- Maya can identify the person or group before sending, joining, or accepting an invitation.
- She can distinguish a direct conversation from a group and find her reply after navigation.
- Required message or group costs are understandable before she authorizes them; declining
  does not silently send a paid action.
- A send awaiting delivery stays visibly pending. An outage does not produce a false success
  or require Maya to decide whether an unseen payment was safe to repeat.
- Labels and recovery guidance let her complete the task without being taught relay hosting,
  RPC settings, nonces, or coin selection. Technical diagnostics may support investigation,
  but they do not substitute for a useful explanation and next action.
